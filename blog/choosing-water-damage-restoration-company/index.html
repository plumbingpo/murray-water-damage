Selecting a Restoration Professional in Murray, UT

Home / Blog / How to Find the Right Restoration Company in Murray, UT

After water damage at a Murray, UT property, few choices matter more than which restoration company you hire. That choice affects the speed of the restoration, how completely the damage gets fixed, what comes out of your own pocket, and whether mold or other secondary problems appear months down the road. Not every outfit is qualified, and hiring the wrong one can mean inflated invoices, a job left incomplete, or something worse. Use the points below to tell a strong restoration company apart from a weak one.

Confirm the Crew Is IICRC Certified

Restoration credentials are measured against the IICRC, short for the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification. Technicians who hold it have gone through demanding coursework on the science of water damage restoration: correct extraction, structural drying methods, measuring moisture, and preventing mold. Before hiring anyone, ask if the technicians carry current IICRC certifications in both Applied Structural Drying (ASD) and Water Damage Restoration (WRT). Steer clear of any firm unable to show proof of them.

Ask for Proof of Insurance and Licenses

Any company you let work in your home needs full insurance, meaning general liability as well as workers' compensation. Have them send certificates of insurance and confirm the dates are current. Without proper coverage, you could be liable for injuries or additional property damage that occur during the restoration. Licensing matters too, so confirm the firm holds whatever business licenses Murray or Utah requires.

Ask How Fast They Respond

A water loss gets worse the longer it waits. Every hour of delay increases damage and cost. Real 24/7 emergency service, with crews that reach your neighborhood fast, should be part of the deal. Get a specific answer on how long they take to reach Murray, and make sure a live person picks up the emergency line instead of voicemail or an answering service that slows things down.

Look at Their Equipment and Drying Process

Good restoration work depends on professional-grade equipment. Three questions cover it: do they use thermal imaging to detect moisture, are their dehumidifiers LGR units (the industry standard), and is extraction done with truck-mounted equipment, which outperforms portable-only operations? Ask how drying is monitored too, because a reputable company checks moisture every day and repositions equipment according to the readings instead of setting it up and walking away.

Look Into Reviews and Past Customers

A company's history shows up clearly in its online reviews. Search Yelp, Google, and the BBB to see if customer satisfaction, professional conduct, and work quality hold up consistently. The most useful reviews talk about communication, punctuality, help with insurance, and how the finished restoration turned out. Don't hesitate to ask for references from recent water damage projects in Murray.

Get Clear on Prices and Insurance Billing

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Xactimate and similar industry-standard pricing programs, recognized and accepted by insurance carriers, are used by many quality contractors. Find out if they deal with insurance carriers directly and can send the bill straight to your insurer. A demand for big upfront money, or a refusal to deal with your insurer, is a warning sign.
