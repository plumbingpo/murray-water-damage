Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Every hour that water sits on surfaces, it penetrates deeper into materials, soaking through carpet into padding, wicking up drywall, saturating wood framing and seeping into subflooring. Murray Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Murray using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Before drying starts, we use specialty tools to pull as much moisture as we can out of those hidden spots, so structural drying takes far less time and equipment and secondary mold is less likely.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Program (781) 527-9564 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule

How to Tell You Need Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Drips or a steady stream coming out of a ceiling, a wall or a fixture
  • Moisture appearing along the bottom of walls or at cabinet toe kicks
  • Seams in the floor that ooze water under foot pressure
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
